## Design Context

### Users

Parkour and freerunning viewers discovering films, documentaries, series and the
people behind them. They should be able to explore the archive without an account.

### Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, community-minded. Preserve the film culture and give creators credit.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user selected a quiet, editorial film reference for the discovery entrance.
Use readable prose, strong editorial hierarchy and restrained JUMPFLIX red.

### Design Principles

- Give real films and their creators precedence over decoration.
- Make useful content and navigation available without JavaScript.
- Keep statistics factual and explain what they count.
- Keep long title lists readable on phones and desktop.
- Connect discovery pages to the existing watching experience.
